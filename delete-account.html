Delete Your Account

Last updated: October 18, 2026

K-9 Handler Academy is an app published by Sophos Solutions Lab, Inc. This page explains how to delete your account and the data that goes with it.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open K-9 Handler Academy and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and open the User tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm twice.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Request deletion without the app

Email support@k-9handleracademy.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm. We only act on requests sent from the account’s own email address.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in: email address, password and display name
  • Your profile and dog profiles
  • Training sessions, drill progress, belts and points
  • Your notes, coach conversations and coach memory
  • Session analyses and drill feedback
  • Your credit balance and usage history (unused credits are lost)
  • Any reports you made about AI replies

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records. Apple (App Store), Google (Google Play) and our subscription processor, RevenueCat, keep their own records of your subscriptions and purchases for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Deleting your account does not remove them.
  • Cached coach audio. Spoken versions of coach replies are cached without your name or account, and expire automatically within 30 days.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it first in your App Store settings (iPhone) or in Google Play (Android) so you are not charged again.
